## Experience

### Reticket

2025 – now

Founder

Reticket started after I saw how difficult it was to trust someone selling a ticket online.

We’re building a safer way to resell tickets. Sellers prove ownership using a ZK proof, buyers pay through the platform, and payouts are released after the event.

It’s live on web, iOS, and Android, with 5K+ signups, a 2K-member WhatsApp community, and $20K+ worth of tickets sold.
